About Me

I started as a manual tester 8 years ago, clicking through apps to find bugs. Now I write code that tests code—automating thousands of test cases that run 24/7 to catch bugs before users do.

After my manual testing days, I moved to a new company where I discovered automation with Katalon. That's where I began automating Web applications, Mobile apps, and APIs—learning the fundamentals of building reliable test suites that could run independently.

Now, my daily work revolves around Appium for mobile automation. I work at a fintech company where the apps I test serve millions of users—ensuring every transaction, payment flow, verification method, and KYC process works flawlessly. I make sure the app is ready and stable before each release reaches users' hands.

I believe my skills can always be upgraded. That's why I'm now diving deep into IT Security—a field closely tied to quality. After all, a high-quality app isn't just bug-free; it's also secure and protects users' data and trust.
